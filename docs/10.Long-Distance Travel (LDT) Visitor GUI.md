# Long-Distance Travel (LDT) Visitor GUI Widget User Guide

## Introduction
The LDT Visitor widget models long-distance travel specifically for out-of-state (non-Florida) US visitors within the Turnpike State Model (TSM). This setup uses unique parameters tailored for visitors distinct from Florida residents.

**Note:** Visitor in this context refers explicitly to Out-of-State (non-Florida) US residents. This is different from Short-Distance Travel (SDT) visitors who include both Florida residents and US residents.

---

## GUI Overview

### Input Configuration

- **Input Directory:** Directory with visitor-specific input files.
  - Interaction: Click **Browse** to choose the directory.

- **Road LOS (Skim):** Visitor-specific road skim file.
  - Interaction: Click **Browse** to select the skim file.

- **Rail LOS (Skim):** Rail skim data for visitor travel.
  - Interaction: Click **Browse** to choose the skim file.

- **Air LOS (Skim):** Air travel skim file relevant to visitors.
  - Interaction: Click **Browse** to select the file.

- **Land-use (FL/US):** Combined Florida and US land-use data.
  - Interaction: Click **Browse** to select the appropriate data file.

- **Synthetic HH (FL/US):** Synthetic household file representing visitors.
  - Interaction: Click **Browse** to select the file.

- **Number of Households (US-FL):**
  - Interaction: Enter manually or use **Auto compute** to auto-generate from synthetic household data.

---

### Output Configuration
- **Output Directory:** Directory where visitor-specific model results are saved.
  - Interaction: Click **Browse** to select or enter manually.

---

### Calibration Settings
- **External Station Counts (Count Year & Future Year):**
  - Define external station count year and future projection year.
  - Enter counts and compound annual growth rates (CAGR) for I-75, I-10, and I-95.

---

## Execution and Saving
- Click **Run** to start the LDT Visitor model execution.
- Click **Save** to preserve your settings or **Cancel** to discard them.

---

## Additional Notes
- Confirm the selected files are correct and tailored specifically for visitor analysis.
- Ensure calibration settings accurately reflect external travel growth expectations.
